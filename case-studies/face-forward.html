Face-Forward: attendance with anti-spoofing

A face-recognition employee attendance system with secure punch in and punch out. Winner at an office hackathon (team of four).

RoleLead engineer, team of 4
ResultHackathon winner
FocusComputer vision, security
CodeGitHub

The problem

ID cards, fingerprints and manual registers are slow and easy to cheat (buddy punching). The goal was a contactless attendance system that HR can trust, and that resists someone holding up a photo of a colleague.

What I built

  • Punch in and punch out with face recognition in the browser, using face-api.js and TensorFlow.js.
  • Anti-spoofing checks so a photo or a screen does not pass.
  • Face embeddings stored in MongoDB with vector search for matching.
  • Admin dashboard with attendance reports, late arrivals, early exits and absentees; exports to CSV, Excel and PDF.
  • JWT authentication, hashed passwords and email alerts.

Architecture

Camera in browser→Face embedding→Anti-spoof check→Vector match→Attendance log

Design decisions

  • Run detection in the browser. Keeps latency low and avoids streaming video to a server.
  • Treat spoofing as a first-class requirement. A recognition system that a photo can fool is not secure.
  • Store embeddings, not photos, to reduce the sensitive data held.

What I would improve

  • Measure false accept and false reject rates on a labelled test set instead of relying on demos.
  • Add liveness checks that are harder to bypass, and a human review path for low-confidence matches.
  • Add consent, retention limits and deletion for biometric data.
