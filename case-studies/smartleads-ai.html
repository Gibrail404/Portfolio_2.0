SmartLeads-AI CRM: Gemini lead scoring

An AI-powered CRM that captures and scores leads, schedules follow-ups and shows performance on a dashboard.

RoleSolo build
FocusLLM scoring, delivery
StackMERN, Gemini API, Docker
CodeGitHub

The problem

Sales teams lose time deciding which leads to chase first. The goal was a CRM that ranks leads by likely conversion so people spend time where it matters.

What I built

  • Lead management with search, multi-criteria filters and pagination.
  • AI lead scoring with the Gemini API that predicts conversion probability for each lead.
  • Interactive dashboard for lead counts, AI scores, conversion rates and KPIs.
  • Role-based access with JWT for admins and team members, and follow-up scheduling.
  • Docker and a GitHub Actions pipeline: build and test on push, deploy frontend and backend with health checks.

Architecture

Lead data→Gemini scoring→Scores stored→Ranked dashboard

Design decisions

  • Show the score next to the evidence. A number alone is hard to trust, so the lead details stay visible beside it.
  • Automate delivery. A CI/CD pipeline with a health check means every change is built, tested and verified before it goes live.

What I would improve

  • Evaluate the scoring against real conversion outcomes with a labelled set, instead of trusting model output.
  • Add a rationale for each score and a feedback button, so users can correct wrong scores.
  • Log prompts, scores and cost per lead for tracing and monitoring.
