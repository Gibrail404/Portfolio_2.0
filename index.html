Four rules I do not skip

Most agent failures I have seen were not smart-model problems. They were missing checks.

1
Verify before reportingSave, generate, check the output, apply, and only then say it worked.
2
Ask, do not guessWhen unsure, the agent asks a structured question with options and a response ID.
3
Approval before livePreviews first, a person approves, then it ships. One gate removes a class of incidents.
4
Evals for every failureEach bug becomes a test case, so the same failure never comes back unnoticed.
